Yearring Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Yearring ("이어링", "年輪") is a habit tracker made by Seonghun Lee ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Yearring apps for iPhone, Apple Watch and Android.

The short version

Your habits stay on your device. We don't run servers for Yearring, we don't have accounts, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Data stored on your device

  • Habits you create (name, icon, color, schedule, goals, reminder time) and their history (check-ins, rest days, quit dates and restarts), plus your app settings.
  • This data is stored only on your device (and on your paired Apple Watch, which receives a copy directly from your iPhone). It is included in your device's own backups (iCloud / Google) if you have those turned on; we cannot access them.
  • Exporting creates a JSON file that you choose where to save or share.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data.

Purchases

If you buy Yearring Premium, the payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). To check whether your purchase is active we use RevenueCat, Inc., which receives your purchase receipt and an anonymous app user ID generated on your device. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Children

Yearring is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can export, edit or delete your data at any time in the app. For purchase-related data held by RevenueCat, contact us and we will request its deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.

Yearring(이어링) 개인정보 처리방침 (요약)

Yearring의 습관 기록은 사용자의 기기(와 연결된 Apple Watch)에만 저장됩니다. 계정, 서버, 분석·광고·추적 도구를 사용하지 않습니다. 프리미엄 구매 시 결제는 Apple/Google이 처리하며, 구매 확인을 위해 RevenueCat이 영수증과 익명 사용자 ID를 처리합니다. 앱을 삭제하면 기록도 삭제됩니다. 문의: sanddeuts@gmail.com

年輪 プライバシーポリシー（概要）

年輪の記録はお使いの端末（とペアリングした Apple Watch）にのみ保存されます。アカウント、サーバー、分析・広告・トラッキングツールは使用しません。プレミアム購入時の決済は Apple/Google が処理し、購入確認のため RevenueCat がレシートと匿名ユーザーIDを処理します。アプリを削除すると記録も削除されます。お問い合わせ：sanddeuts@gmail.com
